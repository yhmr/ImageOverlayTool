# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-13

## 1. Overview

ImageOverlayTool is a desktop application for image overlay and measurement work.  
This application is designed for local/offline use and does not provide a built-in account system.

## 2. Data We Process

The app may process the following data on your device:

- Image files you open in the app
- Project files (`.iot`) you save/load
- Local settings such as language, window size/position, and UI preferences
- Local application logs (when logging is enabled by your usage)

## 3. Data Collection and Transmission

- The app does not send your project/image data to the developer's server by default.
- The app does not include built-in analytics or ad tracking.
- Data is processed and stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export/share files.

## 4. Local Storage

The app stores configuration and operational data in OS-managed local application directories.  
You can remove this data by uninstalling the app and deleting app data folders from your OS profile.

## 5. Third-Party Components

The app uses open-source dependencies.  
Third-party license information is bundled in `licenses.json` in distributed builds.

## 6. Security

Reasonable technical controls are applied in the app (for example, renderer isolation and restricted local file protocol handling), but no software can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically directed to children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app behavior or legal requirements change.  
The latest version date is shown at the top of this page.
